# Backlog items built on 2026-11-22 and 2026-11-23 (emergency branch)

Full text of 9.25, 9.29, 9.30, 12.9, 12.10, 12.12 and 12.13 as they stood in the [product backlog](../PRODUCT_BACKLOG.md) when they were built on `lighthal9-responsive-emergency` and merged into `lighthal9-responsive` (1fbab4a). What was built and what changed at the merge: [emergency merge record](emergency-merge-2026-11.md). Implemented behaviour: [features.md](../features.md).

- **9.25** Other reason fields that require 8 characters (People & Accounts, Face Enrolment Flags, Audit Trail revoke): check each tells the user the minimum before they press the button, as the Secretary attendance change request now does (built 2026-11-20, on production 2026-11-21, see "Done").
- **9.29** Grades page layout (device audit during 13.8, 2026-11-21; not caused by 13.8): on Assessments Manager at Desktop 1920 the sticky page header covers the S.Y. select of the class picker; on the iPhone 393 Grade Weights Editor tab the card title "Configure Grading Weights & Schema" overlaps the Reload button. One `ui-fix-demo` Route B batch.
- **9.30** Dean Grade Submissions is empty when Academic Terms has no row for the current term (found on the demo 2026-11-21, sha c17ad942: the demo has no Academic Terms at all). `handle_admin_grade_submissions` (`backend/controllers/GradeSubmissionController.php`) picks the term and matches classes only through `academic_terms`, while the Faculty side falls back to the class's own term dates. Proposed fix: when no Academic Terms row matches, use the classes' own school year, semester and term dates; reproduce first with a `tests/database` test (bug routine).
- **12.9** Face revoke approval, blocking non-matching updates, and repeat-revoke flagging (Owner, 2026-11-22): rework 12.2's "flag, never block" model. Student face revocation requires an 8–240 character reason and Superadmin approval (face stays usable for check-in while pending; Student can cancel while pending; starting "Update my face" while pending prompts and cancels the pending request; Superadmin sees the reason and approving physically deletes the encrypted template under right-to-delete); 3+ approved Student revokes in a term raise an abuse flag (F3); "Update my face" blocks non-matching faces (discards new template, keeps existing face, tells Student "if this is you, please request a revocation instead") and raises a blocked-mismatch flag (F2); Superadmin gets one unified tabbed Face Enrolments page (Revoke Requests default, Flags) and Overview tile + Needs Attention row. Contract: [face-revoke-approval.md](../contracts/face-revoke-approval.md) (agreed in `/grill-me` 2026-11-22; `spec.md` BIO-002/005/009 and ADM-001 updated).
- **12.10** Close consent-withdrawal instant-deletion loophole (Owner, 2026-11-22; implemented 2026-11-23): `PUT /api/student/biometric/consent` with `{ granted: false }` no longer deletes `.sface` in the sidecar; it expires past-term profiles first, refuses active enrollments with `409 revocation_approval_required` and in-flight enrollments with `409 biometric_enrollment_in_progress`, and preserves pre-enrollment refusal (`consent_status = 'declined'`, keeping `enrollment_status` truthful). Contract: [face-revoke-approval.md](../contracts/face-revoke-approval.md); `spec.md` BIO-002 updated.
- **12.12** Gate Final Retention Status & external final grades behind Submit grades (Client/Owner, 2026-11-23; implemented 2026-11-23): Faculty keep seeing live computed grades and retention previews inside Grade Computation; Students, Dean Dashboard/Reports, Retention Monitoring, Faculty Reports, and Email Management see final course grades and Retention Status (`Remedial` / `Irregular reenrollment`) only after `Submit grades` (GRD-004). The Submit grades confirmation dialog also lists students entering Remedial / Irregular reenrollment; `"Remedial required"` / `"Remedial no longer required"` in-app notifications fire on Submit/Re-submit and Dean-approved Remedial transitions (never auto-save). 24-hour Withdraw stays allowed even after a remedial attempt is recorded (attempts are never deleted and restore on re-submit; new remedial writes are refused while withdrawn or during an open Dean grade-change draft). Contract: [grade-release-gates.md](../contracts/grade-release-gates.md); `spec.md` GRD-004 & UI-003 updated.
- **12.13** Publish Midterm advisory via unified lifecycle banner (Client/Owner, 2026-11-23; implemented 2026-11-23): keep Grade Computation's class-picker bar unchanged and reuse `GradeSubmissionBanner` as one unified lifecycle banner (`Publish Midterm advisory` → `Unpublish` / `Update advisory` → existing 13.8 Submitted states). Midterm scores stay auto-saved and unlocked, backed by migration `073` publish snapshot (`midterm_published_at`, `midterm_published_by`, `midterm_published_snapshot`) so edits never silently flip At-Risk without confirmation; confirmation modal lists At-Risk students + missing Midterm scores (and added/cleared diff on Update) and sends in-app notices (emails stay manual per EML-001). Students see Midterm % and Midterm Advisory only once published, and At-Risk stays visible until Final `Submit grades`. Contract: [grade-release-gates.md](../contracts/grade-release-gates.md); `spec.md` GRD-004 & UI-003 updated.
