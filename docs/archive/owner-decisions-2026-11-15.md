# Owner decisions, 2026-11-15 (backlog 9.4, iBU look)

Ticket wording: "Make report (export) layout more faithful to iBU's format" (reference: a photo of the iBU faculty portal's Grade Encoding page, sent by Micaiah). iBU is Bicol University's portal; "format" means its look, not an import format (BU's import rules are unknown).

| # | Item | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 19 | Grade Summary columns | The Faculty Reports → Grades & Assessments Grade Summary sheet and its PDF section use iBU's columns and order: Student Number, Full Name, Status, Midterm, Tentative, Final, Completion, Notes. Midterm, Tentative and Final are grades (MG, TFG, FG equivalents), not percents. |
| 20 | Status | Passed (final grade below 5.0) / Failed (5.0) / INC (a score is missing) / blank while grades are pending. Chosen over the old Remarks words (Pass, Review retention, Irregular reenrollment). |
| 21 | Completion | The recorded remedial or cost-recovery result; blank if none. The original Final stays unchanged. |
| 22 | Notes | A short automatic note (for example "Remedial attempt 2 passed", "Missing Finals scores"); blank when there is nothing to say. |
| 23 | Look | iBU colours on every export (Excel and PDF), not only Reports, for one formal look. Layouts and data stay as they are, so the official grade workbook still imports. |
