## 3. Decisions already approved (now in spec.md)

The spec amendments are A1–A16 (see `spec-amendments-2026-09-29.md`). In short:

- **GRD-001 transmutation:**
  - The attendance link is optional. Without a link, the score is transmuted and attendance is ignored. Remove the old exam-date auto-match and its warnings.
  - With a link, a missing record means incomplete while the session is open, and Absent after it ends.
  - A revoked linked session counts as unlinked.
- **Retention trigger:** fixed at 2.5 (UI-003). The remedial pass mark is 50%; it may change later.
- **Risk levels (Low / At Risk / High):** projection by assumed 75% assessments. High = 1–2, At Risk = 3–4, Low = 5+. This replaces any mapping from Warning/Critical.
- **Grade weights required** (GRD-002 is now CURRENT). Weights must be greater than 0.
- **REG-008:** one identity kind per person.
- **REG-003:** Google can be linked only after activation, from the profile. Remove Google from ActivateStudent; ActivateFaculty is already password-only.
- **REG-009:** users can never change their own login email. Only pending invitations or records with no account can be corrected.
- **REG-010:** the first Dean invitation is created from `.env` (first-admin email and name) when no Admin exists.
- **REG-006 / BIO-010 Secretary model:**
  - Promote the same Student account.
  - One Secretary per section.
  - Faculty can remove the appointment.
  - The appointment ends with the section or school year.
  - One invitation both activates a new Student account and appoints it.
  - Migrate the existing separate `secretary` accounts into Student accounts plus appointments.
  - A Secretary in Student view gets all of their own Student pages.
  - Use the sidebar toggle only.
- **ATT-002:** embedded OpenStreetMap (Leaflet) location picker.
- **ATT-001 / ATT-003:**
  - Every session has a required class end time, on the same day and at or after the Late cutoff.
  - The session auto-ends at that time and unresolved students become Absent.
  - Faculty get an End Session button.
- **ATT-001 revoked sessions:** ignored for grades, rates and reports, and nobody becomes Absent for them.
- **ATT-006:** build the Secretary Excused request with Faculty approve or reject. The Secretary can no longer set Excused directly.
- **Attendance rate:** Excused counts as attended.
- **DEL-001:** the phone HTTPS dev mode is allowed.
- **EML-001:**
  - Faculty can send At-Risk and Privacy Consent notices from Email Management.
  - With `EMAIL_PROVIDER=custom`, everything goes to Mailpit and only allowlisted addresses also get real e-mail; others are recorded as "Suppressed (test mode)".
- **§8 status:** biometrics is now marked as an implemented research prototype.

- **Open risk (Owner, 2026-09-30): one face enrolled on several accounts ("buddy enrollment").** A student could enroll their own face on classmates' accounts and then check them all in by face. DentiSys cannot detect this today: enrollment and check-in are strictly 1:1 per student (`biometric-sidecar/app.py`), and BIO-001/BIO-007 forbid comparing faces across students. To be addressed in the future; the Owner will decide later. Options discussed:
  - Device binding (no face comparison): a random browser ID registered at enrollment; check-in only from the registered device; one student per device and one check-in per device per session; device changes need Secretary/Faculty approval. Enforcement is per browser, so enrollment through several browsers or profiles remains a gap.
  - Witnessed enrollment: face enrollment only in front of the Secretary or Faculty, recording the witness. Closes the enrollment gap as a process.
  - Detection report: flag several check-ins from the same device or network within minutes (evidence only).
  - Classmate face check: compare a new enrollment with classmates' references and block matches. The only option that proves a shared face, but it requires a BIO-001 amendment, updated consent and re-consent, the BIO-002 privacy review, stricter thresholds (BIO-007), and a generic student message with a private Dean alert; LBPH false matches between similar faces are a real risk.
